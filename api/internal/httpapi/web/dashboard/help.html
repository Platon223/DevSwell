Help

Common questions about how DevSwell works.

What is DevSwell?

DevSwell sends you a personalized digest of changes to your tech stack — releases, breaking changes, and security patches — so you don't have to scroll changelogs and social media to stay current.

How is this different from General News?

Your Home digest is filtered to exactly the technologies you pick on the Stack page. General News is unfiltered — the current top stories from Hacker News, for casual browsing.

How do severity labels work?

Each item is automatically classified as security, breaking, or patch based on keywords in the release notes. Security issues are flagged with the highest priority.

How do I change what I track?

Head to the Stack page and click any technology bubble to select or deselect it, then hit Save. Your digest updates immediately.

How often will I get email updates?

We only email you when something in your tracked stack actually changes — no spam, no fixed schedule of empty updates.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Danger zone → Delete my account. We'll send a confirmation link to your email before anything is actually deleted — nothing happens until you click it.
