Frequently asked

Is DevSwell free?

Yes — creating an account and tracking your stack is free. No credit card required to get started.

What technologies can I track?

A growing list of popular languages and frameworks — pick as many or as few as you want from the Stack page after signing up.

How often will I get emails?

Only when something in your tracked stack actually changes. No spam, no fixed schedule of empty digests.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, from Settings in your dashboard. We'll email you a confirmation link before anything is deleted.
